About this project

Built as an end-to-end Power BI reporting solution for a hotel resort: booking, guest and revenue records were modelled and queried, then shaped into three linked report pages covering front-desk performance, guest profile and distribution channels. DAX measures drive RevPAR, ADR and occupancy calculations, and the segment matrix breaks revenue down to the travel-agent and tour-operator level for commission and channel-mix decisions.
